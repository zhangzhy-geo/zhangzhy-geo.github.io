Visitors

I am a hydrologist and Earth system modeler studying how water, energy, and carbon move between the land surface and the atmosphere. My research combines coupled atmosphere–hydrology modeling, ecohydrology, and model–data fusion with machine learning to understand how terrain, soils, vegetation, and land use shape regional water cycles and ecosystem productivity under a changing climate.

I am a Research Scientist at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I integrate process-based ecosystem models with satellite observations and machine-learning surrogates to constrain water and carbon dynamics at continental scale. Before that, I spent nine years in Germany developing fully coupled, high-resolution atmosphere–hydrology modeling systems for complex terrain and dryland regions across Europe, Asia, and Africa.

Research

Coupled modeling

Coupled atmosphere–hydrology modeling

How do soils, terrain, and lateral water flow feed back on rainfall and land–atmosphere exchange? I develop fully coupled, convection-permitting atmosphere–hydrology simulations with water tagging to trace moisture between land and atmosphere in complex terrain and drylands.

WRF · WRF-Hydro · Noah-MP · water tagging · land–atmosphere interactions

Related publications →
Agroecosystems

Agroecosystem modeling

How do climate, water limitation, and management shape crop productivity? I constrain process-based agroecosystem models with satellite and field observations, using deep-learning surrogates and multi-objective calibration to quantify crop traits and water and carbon dynamics at continental scale.

ecosys · model–data fusion · deep-learning surrogates · phenology · remote sensing

Related publications →
Hydroclimate

Regional water cycle & hydroclimate

Where does precipitation come from, and how will regional water availability change? I quantify moisture recycling, evapotranspiration, and drought propagation by linking high-resolution simulations with observation-based water balance analyses.

moisture recycling · water balance · drought · vegetation feedbacks
